Jungle Drop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Jungle Drop is a one-tap game by Salambek Abubakarov (Norway). This policy explains what happens with data when you play the iOS app or the web version at salambek92-bit.github.io/let-go.

What the game itself collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and we run no servers or analytics. Your best score is stored only on your own device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Ads in the iOS app

The iOS app shows a full-screen ad after every third game. Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC). To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Device identifiers: the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt, and an app-scoped identifier (IDFV);
  • Usage and ad data: ad views and taps, app launches, and performance and crash data related to ads;
  • Coarse location derived from your IP address, plus device type, OS version and language.

This data is used by Google and its partners for third-party advertising, ad measurement and fraud prevention. It is not used by us and is never sold by us. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent (Google's consent form) before personalized ads are shown. You can still play if you decline; you may then see non-personalized ads.
  • iOS asks whether the app may track you. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the IDFA is not shared. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • The web version has no ads and collects nothing.

Children

Jungle Drop is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Sharing

The Share button opens the iOS share sheet with a picture of your score. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose an app to share with.

Your rights and contact

Under the GDPR you can ask about, or object to, the processing of your data. Because we hold no data about you, requests about ad data are handled by Google: My Ad Center. For questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/salambek92-bit/let-go/issues.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
