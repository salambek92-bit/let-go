Let Go — Support

How to play: you hang on a swinging vine. Tap to let go. If your hands hit the next vine you grab it automatically. Hang too long and the vine snaps. Miss, and the crocodiles win. Score = vines caught.

Pass the phone: two players get the exact same jungle, one after the other. Most vines wins.

Ads: the iOS app shows one full-screen ad after every third game, never during a swing.

Reset your best score: delete and reinstall the app.

Problems or ideas? Open an issue at github.com/salambek92-bit/let-go/issues.
